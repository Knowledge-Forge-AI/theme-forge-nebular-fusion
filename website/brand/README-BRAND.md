# Nebular Fusion brand guidance

Nebular Fusion is the Theme Forge desktop workbench for inspecting paired brand systems. The public wordmark is **Nebular Fusion**, with **THEME FORGE** as its product suffix and `✦` as its compact glyph.

## Selected identity strategy

The selected identity strategy is **Full Terminal Nova**: the wordmark, star glyph, deep-violet surface, warm-gold accent, and paired-system voice are treated as one coherent identity system. This is a brand strategy selection, not a claim that every appearance family is included in the released package.

The released site documents Nebular Fusion 0.8.0 and identifies each installation channel by its matching verified publication record. The appearance catalog is a read-only specimen preview; family collection, application selection, editing and persistence remain outside this static site.

## Usage boundaries

- Keep the wordmark as “Nebular Fusion” and the product suffix as “THEME FORGE”.
- Use the `✦` glyph from the public favicon/preview assets as the compact mark. Do not substitute NovaIngot or imply that this site is NovaIngot.
- Preserve the paired light/dark-system language and the distinction between released software and next-cycle appearance candidates.
- Use the asset inventory in [BRAND-ASSETS.md](./BRAND-ASSETS.md) for exact public files and hashes.
- `social-preview.svg` remains the editable source for the 1200×630 `social-preview.png` metadata delivery asset; this rasterization does not change the identity policy.
- Do not imply endorsement, affiliation, or ownership beyond the published project context.
